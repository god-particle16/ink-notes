Built for writing by hand

  • Pressure-sensitive pens

    Pen and pencil respond to how hard you press, so light strokes stay thin and firm ones get bold. The marker keeps a steady line.

  • Highlighter

    A see-through colour that sits over your writing without hiding it.

  • Two erasers

    Rub out part of a stroke, or remove a whole stroke with one touch. The eraser end of your pen works too.

  • Lasso select

    Circle your writing to move, resize, recolour, copy or delete it.

  • Paper your way

    Pick any colour, with blank, ruled, grid or dotted pages.

  • Pages that stack

    Pages sit one below another. Scroll down to keep writing and add a page whenever you need one.

  • Your own shortcuts

    Change any keyboard shortcut to match how you work.

  • Export anywhere

    Save the whole note as a PDF, one page as a PNG, or a backup file you can import later.

Works with the tablet you already have

Huion, Wacom, XP-Pen, Surface and any other pen that works in Chrome or Edge.

On Windows, switch on Windows Ink in your tablet driver’s settings so pressure reaches the browser.

Your notes stay on your device

Notes save automatically in this browser. There is no account to make and nothing is uploaded.

Use your browser’s install button to add InkNotes to your desktop and it works offline too. Clearing your browser’s site data deletes your notes, so export a backup of anything important.
